- Every inventoried `Color.LightGray` site reads `tvMuted` — a
  color-for-color swap at the same call sites, no composable moves, no
  signature changes.
- The Dead/Failed banners gain the destructive treatment: `tvDestructive`
  text on a destructive-tinted surface (bg-destructive/10 law) with a
  `tvDestructiveBorder` border (border-destructive/40 law). The controls
  scrim 0xCC1A1D24 STAYS — it is not a state idiom.
- `InProgressBar`: the fill accent → `inProgressFill`, the track
  chipColor → `inProgressTrack`; the rounded ends stay.
- The RailRow selection tint `tvAccent.copy(alpha = 0.14f)` becomes the
  #1E293B fill (the `.clink-active` idiom) — the accent blue is never a
  selection wash.
- The skip-chip pastels and the fade repair are task 4.2 — not here.
