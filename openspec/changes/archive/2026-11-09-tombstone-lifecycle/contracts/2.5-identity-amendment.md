## Design pins

- The bridge floor is the PARSER-composed series title only — the single serial-parser title, the leg every settled serial series doc carries at minimum (`plugins/serial-parser/parser.go` composes the cleaned title from `dir_name_raw` through the releasename grammar; `tv-parser` never writes series docs — its Match is episode-gated — so no second parser leg exists on a series doc, and `factNamespaces`' facts law is the fixed order that resolves what does; the joint's bracketed-year rename derives the same cleaned title on both sides). Metadata walk titles (`_view`, other namespaces' walk legs) are NOT bridge legs: a mixed enriched/unenriched pair mismatches that wave and converges on the next settle — adoption re-fires at every settle, so the parser-minimum keeps the law simple and deterministic.
- `dir_name_raw` and the path's last segment are NEVER identity legs: a rename means different raw names by definition — either leg disables adoption on exactly the case this change serves.
- NON-EMPTY on BOTH sides is the anti-degenerate law (design D4 amendment): the falsified reality was `"" == ""` passing the title leg and the key degenerating to `(library, "", season, episode)` — legs-only identity. Empty floor on either side = the D4 non-goal's "no identity facts → no match" — the state stays keyed to the dead id, join-dropped and harmless.
- `GetSeriesByPath` is status-unfiltered (visibility-only) — tombstoned series rows resolve for both sides; a MISSING series row on either side floors "" → no match (honest no-op).
