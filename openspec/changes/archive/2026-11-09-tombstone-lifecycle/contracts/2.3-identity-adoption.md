## Design pins

- The trigger fires at ENRICH settle, not scan persist: a fresh item's doc carries only the assembled base at persist time — the served identity facts (`_view` title floor, episode namespaces) exist only after the parser chain settles the row. Enrich-side firing is what makes ONE rescan converge (the acceptance floor); a scan-side match would re-implement the parser grammar or wait for a second rescan.
- The identity key is the SERVED identity (what every surface composes), never raw filenames: `_view.title.value` floor + EpisodeFacts season/episode through the fixed sources order; absent legs match absent legs.
- MOVE semantics via 2.1's transactions: the live item's rows always win (zero-position slots included — accepted edge: a junk zero-position beacon also suppresses adoption of real progress for that viewer; no recency comparison exists to order events, and deterministic target-always-wins is the pinned trade-off); no ghost stock (source rows deleted); idempotent via the NOT EXISTS guards; the returned moved-row counts are the carry signal — `(0, 0)` = the candidate carried nothing.
- Nil-safe `Deps.Adopt`: every existing Deps construction site (tests, other compositions) keeps compiling and behaves identically when the field is unset.

## Acceptance

- A tombstoned item's progress/ledger rows land on the identity-matched live item within one rescan + queue settle (unit-side here; 2.4 proves it end-to-end).
- No identity match → nothing fires; a `(0, 0)` candidate → no-op skip; conflict → live row wins; failures log-and-skip.
- The three named tests pass; the grep pins the Deps field and the cmd wiring.

## Constraints

- `internal/scan/adopt.go` must not import `internal/enrich` — store + naming + doc reads only; scan's existing `internal/enrich` import (run.go, the `Deps` type) is untouched and adopt.go stays queue-free (never touches `enrich.Queue`).
- The hook is synchronous at settle and bounded (library-scoped candidate walk); no goroutines.
