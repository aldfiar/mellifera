## Design pins

- The map (design.md D6): Space play/pause · ←/→ step the playhead ∓/±10 s · ↑/↓ step volume · F fullscreen · N/P next/prev episode item (resolved through the series' episode list — `useSeriesEpisodes`, the cached query) · 0-9 seek to the matching tenth of the media.
- `applyShortcut` is PURE and table-driven; the watch view owns exactly one keydown listener calling it.
- Keystrokes are ignored while a form field or contenteditable element owns focus.
- Legend: one row under the player rendering the map; desktop (`pointer: fine`) surfaces only, hidden on coarse-pointer (touch).
- N/P act only when the series' episode list resolves the adjacent item — absent neighbor → no-op, no wrap demanded.
