## Design pins

- Classification (design.md D2) is SPA-side on served data: `kind === "chapter"`, both `start` and `end` present, and the served lowercase `title` matches the small lexicon — intro: intro/опенинг/opening; credits: credits/титры/ending/outro/ed. Anything else → no chip (honest absence). The reserved `marker` kind stays untouched.
- Chip copy: «Пропустить вступление» / «Пропустить титры».
- Rendering: soft chip over the video's bottom-right; visible ONLY while the playhead is inside a classified range; fades after 4 s inside the range; returns when a seek lands inside again.
- Activation seeks past the section's end through the Player's existing standard `seekTo` — no new seek machinery.
