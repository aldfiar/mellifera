## Design pins

- Claim hands the descriptor + stamped lease (principal hash, lease_until = now + TTL) and returns not-found when nothing is pending or lease-expired — the store's conditional UPDATE does the exclusive pick; Relay adds no locking of its own.
- Consult(rowID, pluginID, plan): a `done`, unconsumed enrich job for the pair whose stored plan matches the current plan (byte-comparison of the rebuilt plan) returns the stored response and marks the job consumed; any mismatch, non-done state, or missing job is a miss (D4 — safe degradation).
- Ingest(jobID, principalHash, result): refuses a wrong principal or an expired lease (the 409 shape upstream); on done for an enrich job — ClearFailure + Notify(library) so derivation re-runs the pair into the consult; on failed — the round increments; the THIRD failed round records terminal `exhausted` via the standard `terminalCooldown` doubling instead of re-arming the flat RelayCool (D5, ruled OQ-3).
- Search-purpose jobs: Ingest completes them but never touches the ledger; their consumer is the search poll (task 5.1).
