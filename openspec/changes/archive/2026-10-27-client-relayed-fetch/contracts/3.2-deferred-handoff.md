## Design pins

- Handoff and consult live at the runSource call site, NOT inside fetchSource (D4): fetchSource stays a pure executor; the call site owns the policy.
- The deferred outcome is a new outcome kind — neither failed nor succeeded; `runSourceRetryable` returns immediately on it (no backoff, no exhausted write: the Relay's `relayed` row IS the loop-safety write).
- The handed plan is the WHOLE plan — primary + extras, positional, the `FetchedResponse[]` shape Convert already consumes; an extra's network failure defers the run with the primary's result (if any) carried in its slot.
- Nil Relay = relay disabled: the network-class failure keeps today's exact behavior (chain failure → retry → ledger). This is also the operator kill switch — bit-identical is the bar.
- Consult-before-fetch: a hit feeds the stored response into Convert with the `_stages` fetch entry stamped `executor: "client"`; a miss (plan mismatch, non-done, absent) fetches server-side as always.
