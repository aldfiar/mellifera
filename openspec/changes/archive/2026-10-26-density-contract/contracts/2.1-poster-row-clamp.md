## Design pins
- The ul emits BOTH tier budgets inline from the pure core: `--poster-track-w-1 = posterCardMaxWidth({chromeH: SERIES_GRID_CHROME_PX, rows: 1, cardBodyH: 60})`, `--poster-track-w-2` with `rows: 2` — custom properties are not in CSSProperties, hence the commented cast at the site.
- `gridTemplateColumns: posterTrackColumns()` = `repeat(auto-fill, min(var(--poster-track-max), calc(50% - 6px)))` — as many fixed tracks as fit, each capped at half the container minus half the 12px gap, so phones keep two columns.
- index.css picks the tier on `.series-poster-grid`: `--poster-track-max: var(--poster-track-w-1)` by default; `@media (min-height: 662px)` raises it to `--poster-track-w-2`. 662 = SERIES_GRID_TWO_ROW_MIN_VH (chrome 182 + 2×60 bodies + 3×120 floor); the index.css↔density.ts pair is pinned by an fs-pin test in density.test.ts.
- SERIES_GRID_CHROME_PX = 182 (collection header ONLY — the original 302 double-counted the card bodies, which also ride the rows·cardBodyH leg of posterCardMaxWidth).
- Tracks follow the budget and cards FILL their tracks — the clamp-inside-a-wide-track raggedness cannot recur; the 2:3 ratio is preserved by construction (`.scard-poster` keeps `aspect-ratio: 2 / 3`).
