# 15.4 Body-epoch race guard contract (audit S-3)

## Goal

An in-flight write from a previous collection never lands in or over the
new body: a bodyEpoch bumped at the commits, captured before every
body-write await, checked at every write.

## The law

- a private bodyEpoch counter on BrowseFlow: bumped at the enter() commit
  and at the selectLibrary() commit (the two body-lifecycle commits); no
  state/UI surface carries it — the UI never reads it
- EVERY body-write site captures the epoch BEFORE its await and drops the
  write (plain return) when it no longer matches — the sites: loadItems'
  write (covers loadBody's tv/image legs, pickFolder's success path,
  extendItems), loadBody's serial-branch commit, toggleFacet's landing,
  loadFacets, loadFolders, openSeries' two commits AND its positions write
  (:718 — it follows the loadPositions loop await, so capture before the
  positions walk and check at the write; surveyor Q12), selectLibrary's
  failure-path revert (:287-301 — Q12: after a later commit the failing
  coroutine's revert lands A's rows over the fresh body AND reverts
  libraryId; the fast B→C double-switch of §B's leg-3 class reproduces
  through it — the drop-when-superseded law IS the fix), pickFolder's
  failure-path revert (:469-474 — the same class on the folder/items
  cluster), refresh()'s body writes (:772-797 incl. its positions write
  :775 — the same shape after the awaits; the same stale-write class
  through the player-exit path — the same capture/check shape, no new
  mechanism)
- no cancellation, no single-flight — the epoch check IS the guard (the
  audit's smaller-diff direction)
- JVM-pinned in a NEW browse/BodyEpochTest over a SUSPENDING FAKE
  MelliferaApi (gated continuations order the interleavings
  deterministically — MockWebServer cannot order two suspended calls): the
  three S-3 legs each assert the new body ends with ONLY the new
  collection's data; a FOURTH leg (Q12) pins the revert class — a
  failing-site revert resolving after a superseding commit is dropped
  (libraryId and rows stay the new collection's); a same-epoch control
  asserts a write without an intervening commit still lands (no over-guard)
