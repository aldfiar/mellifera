## Goal

Grid arrows move the cursor AND the ring in every mounted CardGrid body: the
gridFocus cursor actuator loses the entry gate; entryLandsOnHome gates only
the fresh body mount's cursor-0 claim.

## The law

- the CURSOR actuator: keyed on (state.gridFocus, state.isRailOpen) with
  isRailOpen REQUIRED — surveyor Q10: the only production mount path is
  activateRailRow and the body composes while the rail is still open
  (selectLibrary commits and awaits loadBody BEFORE the rail close,
  BrowseFlow.kt:664-678), so a gridFocus-only key lets the mount-run's
  re-arming focusUntilAttached steal focus from the open rail's focused row
  for the whole fetch window (breaking the «while the rail is open the grid
  holds no focus» law, BrowseScreen.kt:562); with isRailOpen in the key the
  mount-run is gated off and the landing fires on the rail-close flip —
  doubling the nonce actuator by design, coherent; the gate reads NO
  homeHoldsEntry — every gridFocus change re-lands the ring through
  focusUntilAttached; it lives for the whole body lifetime
- the ENTRY claim: a SEPARATE effect keyed on (mountMarker, homeHoldsEntry)
  (NEVER the cursor key) — surveyor Q11: keying ONLY the mount can never
  re-run the claim when the homeHoldsEntry verdict flips and the
  «resolved-empty home hands it back» clause goes unserviced; keying the
  verdict re-runs the claim on the flip. Post-15.2 semantics: every mount is
  a switch mount with home already resolved, so the claim stands down and
  the activation landing owns the cursor per 8.4 (walk62c tile0 5/5); the
  claim's live role is the home-failed/empty box (the claim lands the
  grid); the «late-resolving home pulls the entry» clause is VESTIGIAL
  (nothing mounts while home loads — the rail has no trigger
  pre-resolution), kept only as the key-flip behavior
- KEY-SHAPE note (keeps the duty's negative grep accurate): the entry
  claim's key must NOT start with state.gridFocus — the duty reds any
  LaunchedEffect keyed `state.gridFocus…` carrying homeHoldsEntry in its
  first lines; the entry claim keyed (mountMarker, homeHoldsEntry) never
  matches that pattern
- CardView.onDirection consumes a law-satisfiable move ONLY while the
  actuation backing it is live — post-split every consumable move lands;
  consumption is never a blind state write
- untouched: the nonce actuator (:580-584), the rail-close transition
  actuator (:594-601), the two-emit law, moveInGrid itself
